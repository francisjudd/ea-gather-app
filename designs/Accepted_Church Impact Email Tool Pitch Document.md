| What is this project?&nbsp;(brief summary/outline)&nbsp; | This project would create a simple online advocacy and communications tool enabling churches across the UK to easily inform their local MP about the practical and social contribution they make within their constituency.   The tool would guide churches through a short multi-step process where they can:identify their local MP through postcode lookup;, provide basic church information;, select from a wide range of community activities and ministries;, add rough participation or engagement figures;, include a short personalised message;, generate and send a professionally formatted email directly to their MP.The overall objective is to help policymakers better understand the breadth of church-led social action taking place across the country, while also strengthening direct relationships between churches and elected representatives.   The tool would function both as:a grassroots advocacy mechanism; and, a data and relationship-building opportunity for future parliamentary engagement and an online catalogue of church activities. See tool mock-up [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gDvmLRZqpTyWvuGaloqfZSKz5mkXeWuw/view?usp=sharing) |
