More Than Sunday – Let your MP know the church cares

So much of the good work happening in and through our churches goes unseen and unrecognised. Every week, churches across the country are feeding families, supporting young people, welcoming the lonely, helping people in crisis and bringing communities together. Yet too often, local MPs and decision-makers simply don’t know the full extent of what is happening on their doorstep.

This is an opportunity to change that. By completing this form, you can help your MP see the difference your church is already making in your community. Your story can help build a picture of the church as a vital part of local community life.

It only takes a few minutes, but it could open the door to a valuable relationship with your MP, create opportunities for partnership and help ensure that the contribution of churches is recognised when decisions about our communities are being made.

You are already making an impact. This is a simple way to make sure your MP knows about it.

**Personal Input Section**

In your own words:

Tell your MP what they wish they knew about your church. This may be how your activities benefit the community, your growth, how the Lord is working in your church, and perhaps, how they could help. Your letter will be most effective with a genuine note of thanks for all the hard work your MP does as MPs rarely receive any praise for their work.

Need a starting point?

Examples:

-   **We’re seeing demand grow for…** our food bank, which now supports more than 50 local families each month.
-   **We partner with the council on…** providing a warm space during the winter, offering hot meals, company and practical support to people who might otherwise be isolated.
-   **A story that stuck with us this year…** was an elderly neighbour who told us that our weekly lunch club was the first place they had spoken to anyone all week.
-   **You could support us by…** helping us make connections with local schools and community organisations.

**More Than Sunday database content wording -**

**Let your MP know what your church is doing in your community.**

Every week, churches across the UK are quietly making a difference — feeding families, supporting young people, tackling loneliness, helping people in crisis and bringing communities together. Much of this work goes unseen by the people making decisions about our communities.

**More Than Sunday** is a simple way to change that.

Tell us what your church is doing, share a story about the difference you are making, and we’ll help you turn it into a personalised introduction to your local MP.

By taking part, you’ll help your MP understand the contribution your church is already making, open the door to future opportunities to work together, and help the Evangelical Alliance build a national picture of the incredible social action happening through churches across the UK.
